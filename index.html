THE SPIRIT

More Than A Celebration

Shakti Chakra is a celebration of the energy, devotion and culture that makes Navratri one of India's most vibrant festivals.

From powerful Garba rhythms to the circular movement of Dandiya, every moment is designed to bring people together.

The Chakra represents unity, movement and continuous energy — the same spirit that drives every night of Navratri.

When Shakti rises, every heartbeat becomes a rhythm and every circle becomes a celebration.
