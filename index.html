About Shakti Chakra

We are creating Gujarat's next cultural landmark.

Every year, Navratri brings people together. SHAKTI CHAKRA aims to unite tradition, design, music, craftsmanship and community into one unforgettable experience.

For ten nights, visitors become part of a larger cultural story — culminating in a planned 1000-dhol synchronized performance presented as a Guinness World Record attempt.
